Manager Dashboard · Metric help

Definitions match the dashboard calculations. All reporting dates and timestamps use Eastern time.

Left before queue
Completed, unhandled inbound voice contacts reported as Customer Left, with no queue-entry evidence and no TransferToDN termination. Percentage = these contacts ÷ all inbound voice contacts × 100. This does not prove the caller was dissatisfied or that self-service failed.
Team abandon · After queue / Abandon rate
Unanswered contacts reported as abandoned after queue entry. Percentage = those abandons ÷ contacts presented to a queue × 100. These two percentage cards use the same calculation.
Average queue wait / Average queue time
Average reported total queue duration for queued contacts whose status is Connected or Ended. Both cards use the same calculation. Missing required durations show Unavailable.
Longest wait · Selected period
Largest reported total queue duration among queued inbound contacts in the selected dates. This is total queue time across the contact, not necessarily one continuous wait in one queue.
Service level · 15s
Handled contacts with a reported queue and total queue time of 15 seconds or less ÷ all inbound contacts with a reported queue × 100. The denominator includes abandoned and still-open contacts. Missing required answered-call wait times show Unavailable.
Calls handled
Inbound contacts Webex marks as handled. Includes answered calls still active and answered calls subsequently transferred; the call need not have finished.
Offered / Call volume
Inbound voice contacts arriving during the selected dates, including contacts that never entered a queue. The offered count beneath Calls handled uses the selected queue when a queue filter is applied.
Estimated FCR · 24h
Completed handled calls with no repeat inbound call from the same caller and the same final wrap-up code within 24 hours after the call ends. Percentage = no matching repeat ÷ evaluated calls × 100. Calls wait the full 24 hours before evaluation. Missing caller identity, final wrap-up, end time, or unresolved follow-up reporting prevents evaluation. Today mode includes eligible calls since yesterday midnight Eastern; historical mode uses the selected dates. This is an estimate of resolution.
Call volume heat map
Inbound voice arrivals grouped by weekday and hour in Eastern time, combined across the selected dates. Darker cells mean more calls.
Hourly call volume
Inbound voice arrivals by Eastern arrival hour, combined across the selected dates. This chart covers all queues.
Agent occupancy · OUCU formula
(Available + Inbound connected + Inbound wrap-up + Outbound connected + Outbound wrap-up) ÷ (Last logout − First login) × 100. Summed for each agent’s telephony sessions. Available counts; idle is excluded from the numerator. Gaps between sessions remain in the denominator. No extra hold, consultation, or conference durations are added beyond what Webex includes in these five duration fields.
Live occupancy / Login span
For a session Webex reports as active in a date range ending today, report time replaces last logout and the percentage is labeled Live. Completed sessions use the latest reported logout. First login is the earliest session login. Updates every 60 seconds in Today mode. Missing data, unconfirmed logout, invalid timestamps, or sessions crossing the selected date range show Unavailable.
Agent occupancy component times
Available is the reported time ready to receive contacts. Inbound connected and inbound wrap-up are the respective inbound session durations. Outbound connected and outbound wrap-up are the respective outdial session durations. These five totals can be smaller than the login span because other states are excluded.
Queue health
Groups contacts by their last reported queue. Offered, handled, abandoned counts and rates use that queue’s contacts. On-call agents and queued-contact columns describe current reporting where available, not completed-call totals. Historical queued-contact snapshots are not reconstructed. Queue totals exclude contacts without a reported queue, so they need not equal all inbound volume.
Transfers out / Not queued
Counts contacts without a reported queue that Webex terminates as TransferToDN. Groups each by the final reported blind-transfer node when activity history is complete; missing node names remain unidentified. A transfer-out count does not confirm the destination answered. The Left before queue count in this section uses completed, unhandled, zero-queue-entry contacts reported as Customer Left. Its scope differs from the overview percentage.
Average talk / hold / wrap-up
Each is the average respective duration among completed, handled inbound contacts in the selected queue and dates. Missing required fields show Unavailable. Active calls are excluded from these averages.
OUCU Agent Details
Embedded Cisco Analyzer report. Cisco controls its sign-in, permissions, metric definitions, and date filters. Dashboard From/To dates do not change the embedded report.
Dates, filters, and refresh
Today starts at midnight Eastern and resets at the next Eastern midnight. From/To dates are inclusive, up to 31 days. The queue filter affects queue-specific operational metrics; overview cards, heat map, occupancy, and hourly volume cover all queues. Webex reporting may lag. Unavailable means the app cannot calculate the metric reliably; it does not mean zero.
